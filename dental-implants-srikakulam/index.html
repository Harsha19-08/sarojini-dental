Dental implants

Dental implants in Srikakulam

Replace missing teeth with fixed, natural-looking ones. Seen by MDS-qualified doctors at Day & Night Junction, open every day from 9 AM to 9 PM.

What it is

A dental implant replaces a missing tooth from the root up. A small titanium post is placed in the jaw, and once it has fused with the bone, a crown is fixed on top. It looks, feels and works like your own tooth.

At Sarojini, planning and the replacement teeth are handled by Dr. Lahari Perepu, a prosthodontist and implantologist. Dr. Vijay Muddada brings oral and maxillofacial surgery experience for cases that need more than a simple placement.

Who it helps

  • One missing tooth, several teeth, or a full arch
  • Loose dentures you would like to replace with something fixed
  • A gap you want filled without grinding down healthy neighbouring teeth

What happens, step by step

  1. Consultation and digital X-ray to check bone and gums
  2. A written treatment plan, including whether the bone needs strengthening first
  3. Implant placement under local anaesthesia
  4. Healing time, usually a few months, while the implant fuses with the bone
  5. Crown or bridge fitted, then regular reviews

Questions

Questions about dental implants

Does getting an implant hurt?

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia, so you should not feel pain during it. Some soreness and swelling afterwards is normal and is managed with the medicines we prescribe.

How long does an implant last?

With good brushing, flossing and regular check-ups, implants can last many years. Gum health and habits such as smoking affect how long.

Am I suitable for an implant?

Most healthy adults are, if there is enough bone and the gums are healthy. Conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes, and smoking, can affect healing, so we assess each person at the consultation.
