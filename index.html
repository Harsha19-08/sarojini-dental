Questions

Dentist in Srikakulam: your questions answered

Where is Sarojini Dental Clinic in Srikakulam?

We are at Day & Night Junction in Balaga, Srikakulam, opposite Sri Rama Tiffin Parlour and beside the HP petrol bunk. The Google Maps link on this page gives turn-by-turn directions.

What are the clinic timings?

We are open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 9 AM to 9 PM.

Do you treat dental emergencies?

Yes. If you have severe tooth pain, swelling, a knocked-out tooth or bleeding after an extraction, call 085006 18926 and we will arrange the earliest slot we can.

Which dentist in Srikakulam does dental implants?

Dental implants are done at our clinic by Dr. Lahari Perepu (MDS, prosthodontist and implantologist) and Dr. Vijay Muddada (MDS, oral and maxillofacial surgery experience).

Do you offer braces and clear aligners?

Yes. We offer metal braces and clear aligners for teenagers and adults. After an examination and X-rays we tell you which option suits your teeth.

Is laser dentistry painful?

Many laser procedures, such as gum reshaping, are gentler than conventional methods and we numb the area where needed. No dental treatment can be promised pain-free, but we work to keep you comfortable.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We look after children from their first tooth, including check-ups, fluoride, fillings and habit advice, and we keep visits short and calm.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form on this page, message us on WhatsApp, or call 085006 18926. The form opens WhatsApp with your details filled in so we can confirm your slot.
